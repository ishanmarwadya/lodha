Why we stage the capital

What we build on Parcel D

Premium, not luxury. Three towers of 28 floors, four homes a floor, so every home is a corner home. Money goes into what buyers see and live with: light, open ground and a room to work from home. Fancy finishes are paid upgrades.

Does it fit the plot?

Homes x size must stay inside the carpet area the case allows.

Choices buyers will notice

  • Corner homes. Light and air from two sides, no dark middle flats.
  • About 75% open ground. Towers sit on a small part of the six acres.
  • A study in every 3 BHK. Built for hybrid work.
  • Clubhouse with co-working. A reason to stay home on work days.
  • EV-ready parking. Cheap now, costly to add later.
  • On-Time Bond. Possession six months inside the RERA date, rent paid if MRL is late.

Who runs Dronapur: a hybrid team

Four MRL leaders keep quality, cash and the customer promise. Local hires bring speed and market knowledge. MRL's own vetted contractors build. A local partner who runs execution would take a cut of profit and push D below the hurdle.

How the model works

Same logic as our Excel model, running in your browser. Case inputs are used as given. Where the case gives a range, we picked a point and say why.

  1. Area. Acres x 43,560 x FSI gives buildable area. 75% of it is carpet, which is what buyers pay for.
  2. Sales. Homes sell on the case timing. Each year's sales use a price that grows every year.
  3. Collections. Buyers pay as floors rise. A Year-1 buyer pays 95% within three years.
  4. Costs. Land in Year 0. Build cost on the case timing, growing 7% a year, plus contingency and marketing.
  5. Returns. IRR on the yearly cash. Then again after the city team. Compare with the hurdle.
